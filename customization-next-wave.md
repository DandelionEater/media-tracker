## Design note

Keep customization useful and restrained. Seenary is an overlay, so controls that affect space, readability, focus, and desktop visibility matter more than decorative options.

## Completed from this list

- Background dim strength: separate desktop dimming from the app surface opacity.
- Animation level: Full, Reduced, and Off motion preferences.
- Window size presets: Compact, Balanced, and Cinematic overlay sizes.
- Remembered window size and position: restore the user's last overlay bounds after app restart.
- Home layout editors: edit modes on the Personal and Discover tabs let users drag sections and shelves into a saved custom order.
- Discover page density/layout preference: choose whether the Discover page uses a roomier or denser browsing view.
- Personal page and My List page density controls: choose Comfortable, Balanced, or Compact card sizing for saved anime shelves and list rows.
- Accent language pass: use the selected/custom accent more consistently for active states, progress, primary actions, selected card outlines, and section icon bubbles without tinting the whole app.
- Clickable character and staff cards: open a local modal with role, portrait, native name, voice actor details, richer profile facts, lazy-loaded spoiler-safe descriptions, and local profile caching when available.
- Shortcut editing: temporarily pause the existing global hide/show shortcut while choosing a new one.
- Refreshed anime details overview: replace the old information table with a modern at-a-glance layout showing contextual runtime, watched and remaining time, completion, series age measured from the anime franchise debut, airing progress, next-episode countdown, directly related titles, enlargable cover and banner artwork, and a wider expandable description beside a compact genres and tags panel.
- Animated Discover spotlight: slides use evenly sized pills while the active one grows slightly and fills with timed progress, and title changes transition through a soft blur while the controls remain sharp and the Animation Off preference remains instant.
- Keyboard-ready search: Enter focuses and selects the navbar search, Enter again unfocuses it, Delete clears the current search, and immediate active styling with visible Delete and close controls makes the keyboard state easy to understand.
- Dual-action trailer previews: hovering or focusing a trailer softens the artwork and reveals icon controls for playing it inside Seenary or opening it in the browser, with an in-app player that fits the available window height and sits over a softly blurred, see-through backdrop that follows the app's rounded shape.
- Navbar styles: choose between an Integrated full-width bar, a Floating inset glass surface, or Minimal separated controls, with visual previews and no changes to familiar navigation or search behavior.
- Clearer Compact mode setting: show its actual on or off state in the Appearance summary, explain exactly what becomes smaller, place its full-width control beside the other global appearance choices, and keep Seenary's dark scrollbar appearance when making it slimmer.
- Position-aware window layouts: keep the search bar anchored in place while changing overlay size and remember the last Custom width and height when switching between presets.
- Browsing card styles: choose Default, artwork-led Immersive, or lightweight Gallery cards across Personal, Discover, and search while leaving My List unchanged.
- Modern My List browsing: show progress, episodes left, airing state, next-episode countdowns, recent updates, personal scores, favorites, format, season, and note previews without opening Edit; filter by status, progress, rating, favorites, or format; and switch between detailed List, artwork-led Grid, and side-by-side Board views.
