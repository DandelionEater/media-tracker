# Seenary 0.1.6 Beta

Tag: `v0.1.6-beta`

## Highlights

This update gives you much more control over how Seenary looks, feels, and uses space. It also brings richer anime details, smarter recommendations, a more useful My List experience, and more dependable imports and account syncing.

## New and improved

- Personalize Seenary with custom accent colors, overlay transparency, background styles, desktop dimming, Compact mode, startup views, and Full, Reduced, or Off animations. Compact mode uses slimmer controls while keeping Seenary's dark scrollbar styling. Violet is now the default accent, and your chosen color appears more consistently throughout the app.
- Choose Compact, Balanced, or Cinematic window sizes, enter a custom size, and have Seenary remember the window size and position you last used.
- Rearrange sections on Personal, Discover, and My List, then choose Comfortable, Balanced, or Compact layouts for each page.
- Choose Integrated, Floating, or Minimal navigation styles, with visual previews in Appearance settings.
- Choose Default, Immersive, or Gallery browsing cards for Personal, Discover, and search results.
- Browse My List as a List, Grid, or Board and filter it by status, progress, rating, favorites, or format. Entries now show useful details such as episodes left, airing information, recent activity, personal scores, favorites, season information, and note previews without requiring you to open Edit.
- See Episodes Seen and Time Watched in your Personal overview.
- Get more varied "Since You Liked" suggestions based on your favorites and scores. Seenary gradually gathers recommendations from different favorites instead of repeating the same source.
- Explore a redesigned anime overview with runtime, watch progress, time remaining, franchise age, airing progress, episode countdowns, related titles, and a wider expandable description.
- Open character and staff profiles from anime details to see portraits, roles, voice actors, profile facts, and spoiler-protected descriptions. Previously opened profiles also load faster.
- Enlarge, zoom, and pan cover or banner artwork, play trailers inside Seenary, or open them in your browser. The player now fits better in shorter windows and uses a backdrop that follows Seenary's rounded shape.
- Discover anime through cleaner, more consistent cards, compact "See all" grids, shorter season labels, and a smoother spotlight with timed progress and gentle title transitions.
- Search more naturally with immediate results, clearer active controls, automatic text selection when refocusing, Enter to focus or leave search, and Delete to clear it.
- Set scores with a live 0-10 slider or keyboard input, clear a score easily, and use 0 as a valid rating.
- Record the hide/show shortcut in a clearer editor with validation, easy clearing, correct modifier ordering, and support for valid Space combinations.
- Use Gaming mode from the tray to temporarily pause Seenary's hide/show shortcut without changing the shortcut you saved.
- See clearer Retry screens when Discover, search, or anime details cannot load, along with more consistent and dismissible messages after list actions.

## Reliability and fixes

- Reduced graphics resource use across anime details and full-screen dialogs by replacing expensive live background blur with lightweight dark backdrops and gradients. Scrolling, list editing, imports, sync activity, and update dialogs should now feel smoother, especially on less powerful devices.
- Imports from AniList and MyAnimeList now handle slow or temporary service problems more gracefully, explain failures more clearly, and let you safely cancel work in progress. Closing an active import now asks for confirmation.
- Improved MyAnimeList matching for short, extended, and alternate titles, missing links, and repeated imports.
- Text and PDF imports are less likely to stall, return an empty preview, or show a browser-style error, especially with large or difficult lists. Their success messages now appear in the correct place.
- AniList login, import previews, and account updates now recover properly when local saving fails or entries include start and completion dates.
- Account syncing now shows live progress, retries queued changes sooner, reports activity and totals more accurately, skips entries that have not changed, and avoids repeating work during the same update.
- Your list can fall back to backup storage on this device if its main local storage is temporarily unavailable.
- Seenary now prevents multiple desktop instances from opening at startup.
- Newly opened Settings sections no longer scroll underneath the navigation bar.
